Three steps to your first appointment

No contract to sign, no setup invoice, no onboarding fee.

1

Set your territory and volume

Pick the counties and ZIP codes you'll actually drive to, your weekly appointment target, and your track. We build the campaigns around those constraints, not the other way round.

Put a card on file

A $1 verification charge confirms the card and nothing else. Appointments bill against it as they're delivered — you see every line item in the app, and there's no charge at all until a real appointment lands.

Your part

Open the app and keep your calendar honest

Your appointments arrive there with full homeowner detail. Keep your availability current, pause when your crews are full, and show up on time. That's genuinely the whole job on your side.
