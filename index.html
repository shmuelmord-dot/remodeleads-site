The model

An app, not an ad agency

You don't hire us to run your marketing. You open the app, set how you want to work, and buy appointments. That's the whole relationship.

No retainer, no monthly fee

No setup cost and no contract to sit inside. You pay per appointment, and nothing else.

We pay for the ads

We run our own brands and our own campaigns. You never fund an ad budget and you never see an ad account.

You buy the appointment

By the time anything reaches you, the meeting is already set with the homeowner. There is nothing left to chase.

Vetting

Every consultation is vetted before it hits your calendar

These are not filters on a form — they're questions a human asks on a recorded phone call. If a homeowner fails any of them, the appointment never gets booked.

Verified homeowner

They must legally own the property. Renters, tenants and "my landlord might pay for it" are screened out.

Active 30–90 day timeline

Planning to actually start inside three months. "Maybe next year" doesn't get an appointment.

Real project scope

Full kitchens, full bathrooms, major renovations. Projects big enough to be worth your drive.

Handyman work filtered out

Vanity-only swaps, minor plumbing, spot repairs and small patch jobs never reach you.

The definition is settled before you start. What counts as a delivered appointment — owner verified, scope real, time confirmed out loud, every decision-maker present — is agreed in writing on the first call. Nothing about it is left to interpretation after the fact.
Exclusivity

The lead isn't exclusive. The appointment is.

Most companies are vague about this, so here is the straight version — it is the part contractors are usually surprised by, and we would rather you hear it from us now than work it out later.

Nobody comes with you

No other contractor shows up to your appointment. The meeting in that home is yours alone — your hour, your pitch, no one waiting in the driveway.

2 to 2.5 bookings on average

A homeowner may meet more than one contractor about the same project — 2 to 2.5 times on average — with enough time between the meetings to give you the full opportunity to close before anyone else is in the room.

One open meeting at a time

A homeowner never has back-to-back meetings booked by us. There is only ever one appointment ahead of them.

Why we tell you this up front. A company that promises a genuinely exclusive homeowner is either charging you several times this price or is not being straight with you. What we can promise is the meeting: it is yours, it is unshared, and you get first run at it.

Setting expectations

It's a numbers game

We would rather you start with the right picture than a flattering one. This is what advertising-sourced work actually looks like.

Not every appointment is the same

Some homeowners are stronger, some weaker. Some jobs are bigger, some smaller. The average is what pays — not any single meeting.

Not everyone tells the whole truth

People don't always give the full picture on the phone. We verify what can be verified, but it's worth factoring in.

Ads aren't referrals

Homeowners who come from advertising are naturally cooler than a referral or word of mouth. Ours are very strong ad-sourced appointments — but they are not referrals, and we won't pretend otherwise.

We do everything we can

Every step, from the ads through to the calls and the confirmation, is built to hand you the best meeting possible. That's the part we control, and we take it seriously.

For $100 on top of the $250 base — $350 in total — we contact the homeowner ahead of the visit, re-confirm the time, verify they'll be there, and keep you updated in real time. If the meeting is canceled or nobody is home, that appointment comes back to you as a credit, applied automatically.
